# Citizen comparative projection pattern — recorded design note

## Observation

The Human QA of **Control preventivo de identidad** found the section
“Control preventivo vs. control investigativo” useful because it makes a legal
boundary visible without treating the two procedures as interchangeable.

## Candidate pattern

The existing generic named public-section projection can render a bounded
comparison when each side is supported by the immutable expedition statements.
This is a presentation pattern, not a new legal or financial data model.

Potential future uses include:

- current regime versus future regime;
- transferred versus rendered versus approved;
- restitution ordered versus recovery evidenced;
- investigation, formalization, and conviction;
- proposal, published law, and entry into force.

## Constraints

Comparisons must name their scope, date or cutoff when relevant, and evidence for
each side. They must not derive arithmetic, legal authorization, or equivalence
from mere visual adjacency.

## Decision

The renderer already supports a minimal generic named section. Do not introduce a
dedicated comparative component, schema, or Core abstraction until multiple cases
require the same structured fields.
